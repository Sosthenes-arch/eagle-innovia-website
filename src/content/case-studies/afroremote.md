They needed one place to find remote jobs genuinely open to them, and a clear signal of what to check before applying.
built: Eagle Innovia designed and built AfroRemote, a web platform that gathers remote job listings and runs each one through an eligibility scoring engine. Every job gets a plain label showing whether candidates in Africa can apply, so they spend their time on roles they can actually get.
